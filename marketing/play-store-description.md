## Marketing copy notes

- The phrase **"who would win"** is the single highest-value search phrase in this niche. It should appear naturally in the full description (it does, in the opening line).
- The category list near the bottom of the full description is for ASO crawlers, not human readers — it captures franchise long-tail queries ("dbz vs marvel", "stranger things fight", etc.) without looking like keyword stuffing because it's framed as a feature list.
- Avoid "Death Battle" by name in the title or short description — it's a trademarked Rooster Teeth IP. Generic phrases ("dream match", "settle the debate", "who would win") are safe.
- Avoid claiming celebrity endorsements, official licensing, or any rights-holder affiliation — the roster is fan commentary / fair use only.
